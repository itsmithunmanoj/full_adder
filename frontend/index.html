PROJECT OVERVIEW

What is a Full Adder?

A Full Adder is a combinational digital circuit that adds three binary inputs: A, B and Carry-in. It produces two outputs: Sum and Carry-out.

SUM

A ⊕ B ⊕ Cin

CARRY OUT

AB + BCin + ACin

LESSON 01

What is a Full Adder?

A Full Adder is a combinational logic circuit. It adds three binary inputs: A, B and Carry-in. It produces two outputs: Sum and Carry-out.

LESSON 02

Full Adder Inputs

  • A: First binary input.
  • B: Second binary input.
  • Cin: Carry received from the previous bit.
LESSON 03

Full Adder Outputs

  • Sum: The result bit of the addition.
  • Cout: The carry sent to the next bit.
LESSON 04

Full Adder Formulas

SUM Sum = A XOR B XOR Cin CARRY OUT Cout = AB + ACin + BCin
LESSON 05

Example Calculation

Let's calculate 1 + 1 + 1.

1 + 1 + 1 = 3 in decimal

11 in binary

Sum = 1 and Carry-out = 1

LESSON 06

Where is a Full Adder Used?

  • Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU).
  • Computer processors.
  • Binary addition circuits.
  • Digital calculators.

About LogicLab

An interactive Full Adder learning and simulation platform.

Project Aim

To demonstrate how a Full Adder adds three one-bit binary inputs and produces a Sum bit and a Carry-out bit through an interactive website.

Subject Integration

  • Digital Fundamentals: Full Adder, Boolean expressions, logic gates and truth table.
  • Computer Architecture: binary arithmetic and the role of arithmetic circuits in a processor's ALU.
  • Data Structures using C: arrays store the eight input combinations and their outputs; linear search can locate a combination.
  • Software Engineering: requirements, design, implementation, testing and documentation.

Technology Used

HTML, CSS and JavaScript for the frontend; Node.js and Express for the backend; C for Full Adder calculation; browser LocalStorage for theme and calculation history.

Full Adder Equations

Sum = A XOR B XOR Cin

Cout = (A AND B) OR (Cin AND (A XOR B))
